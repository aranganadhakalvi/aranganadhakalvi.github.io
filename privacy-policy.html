    Welcome to AranganadhaKalvi — a virtual internship platform designed to help students find and apply for internships across various sectors. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains, in plain language, exactly what we do with your data.

    1. Introduction

    Welcome to AranganadhaKalvi, a virtual internship platform designed to help students find and apply for internships across various sectors. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

    2. Information We Collect

    When you use AranganadhaKalvi, we may collect the following types of information:

    • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, educational background, and other details required for internship applications.
    • Account Information: Login credentials and preferences.
    • Internship Application Data: Details of internships you apply for, tasks submitted, and performance scores.
    • Automated Data Collection: IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies to enhance user experience.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information collected for the following purposes:

    • To create and manage your account.
    • To facilitate your internship application process.
    • To provide weekly tasks and evaluate your submissions.
    • To improve our platform based on user feedback and analytics.
    • To send notifications and updates regarding internships.
    • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

    4. How We Share Your Information

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. However, we may share your information with:

    • Employers / Internship Providers: When you apply for an internship, relevant details may be shared with the internship provider.
    • Service Providers: Third-party services such as the OpenAI API for task evaluation and analytics tools.
    • Legal Authorities: If required by law, regulation, or legal proceedings.

    5. Data Security

    We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    6. Your Rights and Choices

    As a user, you have the right to:

    • Access and update your personal information.
    • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
    • Opt out of marketing communications.
    • Request a copy of your stored data.

    For any data requests, contact us at admin@aranganadhakalvi.com.

    7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

    We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience, track website performance, and remember user preferences. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

    8. Third-Party Links

    Our platform may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

    9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices. We will notify users of significant changes through email or platform notifications.
